HOW TO PLAY

A D / ← → change lane
W / Space jump, again for a double jump
S / ↓ drop fast
P pause · R restart · M mute

HAZARDS
Walls: switch lanes
Low bars: jump over
Laser gates: pulse on and off; jump them or pass while dark (they flicker before lighting)
Sliding blocks: sweep across all lanes; time your pass
SCORING
Chain orbs to raise the multiplier (up to ×5). Near misses give bonus points. Stars come from orbs collected.
